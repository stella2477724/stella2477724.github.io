Privacy Policy / Политика конфиденциальности

Effective date: 30 October 2026

What this app is

Felion Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Felion shop to upload the shop's own videos to the shop's own YouTube channel, Instagram account and VK community. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we access

With the owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and set their title and description on the owner's channel, and to read the channel name to confirm the connection. It uses the Instagram and VK APIs in the same way for the owner's accounts.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository and are used only to publish videos the owner has approved. The app does not collect, sell, share or analyse personal data of viewers or any other people. No data is transferred to third parties other than the platforms the videos are published to.

Google API Services

Felion Publisher's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using the YouTube upload feature you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google), in Instagram settings under Apps and websites, and in VK security settings. Tokens can also be deleted from the repository secrets.
